## Useful read-only checks (no secret needed)

- `GET /api/collector-status` — ticks, coverage, gaps, last success per poller.
- `GET /api/scorecard?days=10` — sections, hit rates, dates with data. Call this before
  `?date=<today>`: a dated call on an unfinalised past day finalises it and sends its Telegram.
- `GET /api/scorecard?date=YYYY-MM-DD&series=1` — one day in full.
- `GET /api/swing` — daily summaries and the outlook.

## Last commit of the day

Four fixes pushed together on the evening of 16-Nov, after the close. The suite stood at 628 tests,
all passing, and the frontend built.

1. **Track Record finalisation guard.** A row is finalised only from NSE's real spot with a data
   time at or after 15:30 (the code used a hard-coded `25030.35` when NSE omitted the value, and
   accepted a stale pre-15:30 response). If no qualifying refresh arrives by 15:50 the row is
   finalised from the best real spot seen and flagged `entryUnverified`; if no real spot was ever
   seen a carry becomes `UNRESOLVED` ("Entry not recorded"). `src/eodTrackRecord.js`.
2. **Fusion scorecard events ignore flicker.** A change of quadrant or scenario is recorded only
   when it differs from the last recorded value and at least 10 minutes have passed
   (`SCORECARD_FUSION_REARM_MIN`, 0 restores the old behaviour). Replaying Monday's per-minute
   ticks gives 13 events against 15, so this removes rapid back-and-forth flips but does not make a
   choppy day quiet. FUSION counts before 17-Nov include flicker. `src/scorecard.js`.
3. **Opening anchor never a previous-session tick.** Ticks before the futures volume reset are
   excluded before the existing basis/OI test. On Monday's real ticks the anchor is still 09:19
   (`volumeResetSkipped: 1`); the rule matters on a day when a stale tick happens to pass the
   basis and OI tests. Without volume data the picker behaves as before. `api/nifty-oi.js`,
   `src/sessionOpen.js`.
4. **GitHub cron** moved to off-peak minutes, every 10 minutes (`7-57/10 3-10 * * 1-5`), and its
   header now says plainly that it is best-effort and has not provided cover.
